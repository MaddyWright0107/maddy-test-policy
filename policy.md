# Maddy Test - Customer Support Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19
Version: 1

This is a made-up policy for testing. Use the latest version for every reply.

## Policy

- **Refunds:** Full refund within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, offer account credit only. Never promise a refund over $200; escalate to a manager.
- **Outages:** Apologise, give the status page link (status.maddytest.example), and never promise a fix time.
- **Privacy:** Never share another customer's information. Do not confirm or change account details until identity is verified (ask for the account number and the last 4 digits of the phone number on file).
- **Legal:** Never admit legal liability or give legal or financial advice.
- **Escalation:** If the customer mentions legal action, the media, or a safety issue, do not draft a reply. Flag it for a human manager instead.
- **Response target:** Reply within 1 business day.

## Writing standards

- Friendly, calm, professional. Plain English.
- Start with "Hi [first name],".
- End with "Kind regards, [Employee name], Maddy Test Support".
- Keep replies under 150 words, in short paragraphs. No jargon, no emojis.
- Apologise once if something went wrong, then move to the solution.
- Banned phrases: "unfortunately", "as per my last email", "calm down", "You are wrong".

## Process

- For every customer email, write a draft reply only. List which policy rules you used.
- Show the draft and wait for approval ("approved" or "approved but change X").
- Never send anything without approval.
